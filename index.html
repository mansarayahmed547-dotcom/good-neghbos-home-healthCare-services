Essential Health Advice for Sierra Leoneans

Malaria Prevention & Care

Malaria remains prevalent. Always sleep under long-lasting insecticidal nets (LLINs). If experiencing high fever, chills, or headache, avoid self-medication; request a Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT) from our home visit team.

Safe Water & Typhoid / Cholera

Ensure drinking water is boiled or treated with chlorine. Maintain strict hand hygiene with soap and water before meals and after using sanitation facilities to prevent food-borne and water-borne illnesses.

Hypertension (High BP) Awareness

High blood pressure often shows no early symptoms. Routine screening is vital. Reduce salt intake, maintain active routines, and let our community health nurses check your blood pressure regularly at home.

Emergency Escalation (117)

For critical life-threatening medical emergencies requiring immediate intensive hospital intervention, please contact the national health line 117 or visit the nearest referral hospital immediately.
